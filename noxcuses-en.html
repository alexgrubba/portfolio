From a commission for an academy to a complete sports brand system. The story of how one project changed direction and became something more.

Brand Identity Sportswear Design Pattern Design Print / Production
01 — Brief

Where it
all started

A coach from the AP Cassubian football academy came in with an idea to create his own clothing brand. The goal was to design a complete match kit consisting of a game jersey, a casual jersey, and shorts.

The first version was created under the Project52 brand, using the academy's colors. During the process, the concept shifted — instead of working under the existing logo, the decision was made to build a completely new, independent brand.

The result was NO EXCUSES — a new visual identity, color palette, logo, and graphic system for sportswear.

03 — Process

How the
logo was made

Exploration — X variants, typography, directions

The starting point was the letter X — short for "excuses". Dozens of variants were tested: outlined, filled, with serifs, geometric, interlocking.

Typography for "NO EXCUSES" was explored in parallel — from serif to condensed bold, from subtle to aggressive typefaces.

The final N/X logo combines the letters N and X cut through by a lightning bolt. Energy, speed, and the character of a sports brand aimed at young athletes.

Final logo version — embroidered on the kit

04 — Visual System

Brand language

Color palette
#222222
#353535
#7C7C7C
#B7B7B7

Full grayscale. Moving away from club colors — here the pattern and logo define the kit, not the color. The monochromatic palette gives it a premium feel.

Typography
NO EXCUSES
ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ 0123456789
Sofachrome Regular — all branding elements

Sofachrome is a font with a sporty, angular character. Used on the collar tape, labels, and all brand communications.

Pattern — lightning bolt system

Lightning bolts as the main motif — a geometric echo of the X in the logo. Pattern sublimated onto the fabric, with a top-to-bottom gradient creating depth and dynamics. The same pattern appears on both kit variants.

06 — Takeaways

What this project
taught me

01

The system matters more than any single element

A logo is just one piece of the puzzle. Only when combined with colors, patterns, and details does it create a cohesive brand identity. This project showed me that a brand works best when every element supports the others.

02

Changing direction is part of the process

The shift from Project52 to NO EXCUSES was a decision about ownership and identity. Sometimes the best project is the one that changes its own direction along the way.

03

Designing for production

A mockup in Photoshop is one thing. Seeing the N/X embroidery on real fabric is another level entirely. Production tests the design mercilessly and teaches you to respect the details.
