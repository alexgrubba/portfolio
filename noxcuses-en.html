01 — Origin and Brief

From Brief
to Revolution

“Authentic brand identity begins where safe compromises end. Rather than another cookie-cutter kit, we engineered an independent statement of athletic determination.”

— Alex Grubba, Creative Direction

A coach from the AP Cassubian football academy initially came in with an everyday task: design a dedicated match kit (home & away) under the provisional working title Project52.

Yet it quickly became evident that this project carried far greater potential than club boundaries could hold. Instead of confining the aesthetic to a local crest, we formulated a bolder strategic thesis: build an autonomous, sharp sportswear brand that young players wear with pride both on the pitch and on the street.

This gave birth to NO EXCUSES (N/X) — an uncompromising philosophy where the razor geometry of the letter X, a proprietary lightning pattern, and strict monochromatic discipline form a cohesive, high-performance ecosystem.

03 — Mark Anatomy

How the N/X
monogram was born

Design workbook — vector explorations, break angles of the letter X, and deconstruction of form

The core focal point was the letter X — the phonetic and visual heartbeat of the word EXCUSES. Dozens of geometric configurations were examined: from aggressive angular cuts to interlocking forms merging with the letter N.

The resulting N/X monogram unifies both characters in a compact glyph intersected by a geometric lightning bolt. The strike is no superficial ornament — it is a vector of speed, relentless focus, and determination for young athletes.

06 — Key Takeaways

What this project
taught me

01

The system carries more weight than any logo

An isolated logo mark never builds an authentic sports brand. Only when woven together with a modular pattern, ergonomic tailoring, and color discipline does a garment project an identity the athlete feels instantly upon putting it on.

02

Courage to pivot mid-course

Transitioning from the tentative Project52 concept into an independent brand was a bold but defining move. Exceptional design work demands the confidence to challenge initial premises when a superior insight surfaces during exploration.

03

Designing for the factory floor

Digital artboards are forgiving; real manufacturing scrutinizes every stitch. Embroidery thread density, sublimation ink absorption, and seam tensile strength taught me profound respect for technical craft.
