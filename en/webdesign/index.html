PROBLEM

Alex Agency required a digital presence that could effectively showcase their portfolio of work while reflecting both professionalism and creativity. The main challenge was to design a platform that communicated their expertise and data-driven approach, while also highlighting the agency's bold, innovative character.

✦
SOLUTION

We designed a website and visual identity that balance professionalism with creativity. Bright color accents and brutalist-inspired details reflect Alex Agency's bold character, while a clean layout ensures clarity and trust. The result is a digital presence that is both distinctive and reliable.
