From handwritten price lists
to an authoritative digital presence

PROBLEM

Studio Bożena possessed decades of craft mastery and a loyal clientele, but suffered from an outdated, fragmented brand image. Loose paper price sheets, the absence of clear hair-length categories, and zero online presence created hesitation for prospective clients and blocked acquisition of younger customer segments.

B
SOLUTION

We developed a complete 360° visual identity: from an elegant calligraphic logotype to modular POS service boards, custom staff apparel, and a high-converting minimalist website. The platform was built with immediate booking conversion in mind, featuring direct click-to-call, embedded Google Maps navigation, and transparent service tiered pricing.

04 • Digital Architecture

Minimalist, conversion-driven web platform

Built as an agile single-page experience optimized for mobile screens. Clients browsing on smartphones instantly access tiered hair-length pricing, past hair transformations, and a direct click-to-call booking trigger.

01 / CONVERSION

One-Tap Booking (Click-to-Call)

The primary call-to-action dials the salon reception directly, minimizing the reservation journey to under 2 seconds.

02 / ARCHITECTURE

Tiered Hair-Length Pricing

Clear categorization across short, medium, and long hair alongside specialized treatments (sombre, keratin, permanent waves).

03 / LOCALIZATION

Google Maps Navigation

Live interactive maps embed with exact coordinates in Gdynia (ul. Stanisława Dąbka 232) and working business hours.
