Visual Identity

✦ Overview • Identity & Brand Assets

We craft branding that does more than catch the eye: it accelerates commercial growth

Visual identity is your company's public face. It is the immediate signal telling buyers who you are and why they should trust you. At Alex Agency, we translate strategic positioning into a cohesive, commanding visual narrative that commands respect across your sector.

By combining core assets (geometric marks, typography, certified colors, and precision prepress), we build brand identities that perform flawlessly across all media: from smartphone screens to industrial wide-format print and physical product lines.

Our methodology goes far beyond surface decoration. We create precision sales instruments that solidify market authority, foster client loyalty, and eradicate technical manufacturing risks.

Key process phases: 01 • Discovery Workshop ↓ 02 • Key Visual & Logo System ↓ 03 • Brand Manual & DTP Prepress ↓ 04 • Motion Design System ↓
01

Discovery
Workshop

A vital onboarding phase to thoroughly analyze your business model, customer segments, and commercial targets.

We examine positioning, direct competition, and operational priorities to establish a rock-solid foundation, ensuring the design process is precise, objective, and tuned to enterprise buyer expectations.

02

Key Visual
& Mark Architecture

The Key Visual is the foundation of your brand's visual presence, present across all touchpoints from website to social channels, print catalogs, and exhibition booths.

We craft proprietary marks and monograms based on strict geometric grids that maintain razor-sharp clarity as a 16px favicon or on an oversized light box. We establish a visual motif ensuring instant recognition.

03

Brand Manual
& DTP Prepress

Technical and visual documentation codifying identity rules: typography, certified color palettes (Pantone, CMYK, RGB, HEX), clear space, and scaling logic.

As DTP specialists, we produce certified production files (FOGRA39, PDF/X-4), bespoke 1:1 die-cuts, and print finish masks (selective UV varnish, hot stamping, embossing). We guarantee 100% color consistency between screen and press.

04

Motion Design
System

A structured library of animation principles and components standardizing brand movement in digital spaces.

We define easing curves, micro-interactions, logo idents, and dynamic layouts for short-form video (Reels, TikTok, DOOH). Motion injects contemporary flair and drives engagement in the video-first era.
